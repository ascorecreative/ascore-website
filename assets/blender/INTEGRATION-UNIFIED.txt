Final richer rendering / live reflection guidance
The earlier pale proof is superseded. Final front swatches include #097F80, #3EA8AA, #096084, #2C5C9A, #333983 and #4E60A5 as chosen per source region, with no emission and no texture. The source Blender preview uses AgX at exposure -1.4 to protect color saturation under studio cards. Blender exposure is not embedded in GLB, and its lights are not numerically equivalent to browser RoomEnvironment. Start with restrained environment intensity (roughly 0.5–0.7) and renderer exposure around 0.8, then compare against rich-center-final.png rather than copying Blender's numeric exposure literally. Keep clear teal/blue body colors between moving highlights; avoid broad white light flooding the whole center.

Move reflection/environment orientation or a soft key light gently with scroll/pointer, preferably around 10–20 degrees, while retaining the accepted blade choreography. This must remain physically rendered lighting on the PBR materials, not an emissive overlay or a CSS shine. Respect reduced motion. rich-center-reflection-final.png uses the same geometry/materials and a deliberately larger light rotation as a visible comparison, not an animation prescription.
